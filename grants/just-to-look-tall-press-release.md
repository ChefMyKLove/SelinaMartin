# Selina Martin and Torrance Release Trans-Atlantic Rock Collaboration "Just to Look Tall"

**Contest-winning single born from a Canada–Spain songwriting challenge tackles Trump’s narcissistic abuse — with a driving indie-pop/rock hook**

VALENCIA, Spain, August 13, 2026 — Canadian singer-songwriter Selina Martin and Toronto-area alternative rock band Torrance today release "Just to Look Tall," a multi-artist trans-continental rock and roll collaboration blending Torrance's driving indie rock with Martin's razor-sharp lyrics and raw, perfect vocal delivery.

The track is available now. \[Streaming/purchase link\]

## The Contest

Tamara Williamson, frontwoman of Torrance and formerly of the 1990s BMG-signed alternative group Mrs. Torrance, launched a songwriting contest with a simple premise: Torrance would supply the music, and entrants would submit their own lyrics and melody. From across the ocean in Spain, Martin submitted her version — and won.

"Just to Look Tall" is the result.

## About the Song

Inspired by the non-stop deluge of craziness coming from the current President of the USA, and fuelled by her own recent experience with someone with Narcissistic Personality Disorder (NPD), Martin wrote the lyrics for "Just to Look Tall" to express a profound frustration, to inform listeners that the behavior is pathological in nature, and to remind them that it does not get better until you walk away.

"I used to know nothing about NPD," Martin said. "I thought narcissism was simply an unpleasant but benign trait — like a narcissist is someone who is a bit overly vain, no big deal. I had no idea how dangerous and detrimental these individuals are, until I learned the hard way. And now I know a lot.

"At the moment the entire world is suffering through a relationship with someone who has NPD. People with true NPD, and it's painfully clear that Trump is one, are untrustworthy, manipulative, and are not capable of taking responsibility for their actions. They're never accountable for bad — or even illegal — behaviour. Ever. Instead they'll find a way to blame someone else, or you.

"Not only this, but they're not capable of love, compassion or empathy. It's actually impossible for them to experience these feelings. And a leader of a people who is incapable of having empathy for his people is a dangerous thing."

Chances are the listener also knows at least some of what Martin is talking about — close to 60% of the general population has been affected by a relationship like this, at least to some extent. Exact numbers are hard to pin down, as it's extremely rare for people with this disorder to seek help and get a proper diagnosis.

"The only remedy is to not react, to grey rock, to walk away, to placate if necessary but do not engage," Martin said. "This takes some doing because they cannot stand being ignored, which is why DT literally has to make headlines every day. It's part of the pathology."

On the title, Martin added: "I know Trump is physically tall. But I also know he is driven by an underlying monumental insecurity; an inner smallness. The song title is a metaphor."

So — while we endure what feels like the weirdest, most globally inhumane time in human history, "Just to Look Tall" offers a driving, melodic indie-rock/art-pop trans-Atlantic Canadian collaboration: two minutes and thirty-seven seconds of joyous release.
