# Just to Look Tall — Release Copy
**Selina Martin & Torrance**

---

## Punch Line

Tired of Trump? Here's your tonic!

Just to Look Tall, a brand new song by Selina Martin and Torrance, is a multi-artist trans-continental rock and roll collaboration!

The combination of Torrance's driving indie rock with Martin's razor-sharp lyrics and raw, perfect vocal delivery makes this a must-listen for anyone needing an energy boost — and a shot of camaraderie, at a time when world leaders are deliberately trying to divide in order to conquer.

---

## Release Description
Tamara Williamson of the Canadian indie rock band Torrance started a contest:

We make the music, YOU submit your lyrics and melody, and let's see who does it best.

From across the ocean in Spain, Martin submitted her version and won the contest!
And Just to Look Tall is the result.

Torrance is an alternative rock band from the Toronto area. It is fronted by singer and songwriter [Tamara Williamson](https://www.tamarawilliamson.ca/), who previously led the 1990s BMG-signed alternative group Mrs. Torrance. [https://www.tamarawilliamson.ca/about-4](https://www.tamarawilliamson.ca/about-4)

Original cover art for "Just to Look Tall" was created by Tamara Williamson of Torrance.

Selina Martin is an innovative Canadian post-pop/rock songwriter and musician currently based in Europe. Her musical style evolves album to album and her work has been consistently highly acclaimed by critics on both sides of the Atlantic. [https://selinamartin.com](https://selinamartin.com)

---

Inspired by the non-stop deluge of craziness coming from the current President of the USA and fuelled by her own recent experience with someone with Narcissistic Personality Disorder (NPD), Martin wrote the lyrics for Just to Look Tall to express a profound frustration, to inform us that it's pathological in nature, and to remind us that it does not get better until you walk away.

"I used to know nothing about NPD," Martin says. "I thought narcissism was simply an unpleasant but benign trait — like a narcissist is someone who is a bit overly vain, no big deal. I had no idea how dangerous and detrimental these individuals are, until I learned the hard way. And now I know a lot.

At the moment the entire world is suffering through a relationship with someone who has NPD. People with true NPD, and it's painfully clear that Trump is one, are untrustworthy, manipulative, and are not capable of taking responsibility for their actions.

They're never accountable for bad — or even illegal — behaviour. Ever. Instead they'll find a way to blame someone else, or you.

Not only this, but they're not capable of love, compassion or empathy. It's actually impossible for them to experience these feelings.

And a leader of a people who is incapable of having empathy for his people is a dangerous thing."

So! While we endure what feels like the weirdest, most globally inhumane time in human history, let's enjoy this driving melodic indie-rock/art-pop trans-Atlantic Canadian collaboration!

It's 2 minutes and 37 seconds of joyous release. Give it a spin!

NB. Re: the title of the song, Martin also says: "I know Trump is physically tall. But I also know he is driven by an underlying monumental insecurity; an inner smallness. The song title is a metaphor."

Chances are the person reading this also knows at least some of what Martin is talking about, as close to 60% of the general population has been affected by a relationship like this, at least to a certain extent. Exact numbers are hard to know, as it's extremely rare for people with this disorder to seek help and get a proper diagnosis.

Martin says: "The only remedy is to not react, to grey rock, to walk away, to placate if necessary but do not engage. This takes some doing because they cannot stand being ignored, which is why DT literally has to make headlines every day. It's part of the pathology."
